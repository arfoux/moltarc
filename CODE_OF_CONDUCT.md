# Code of conduct

Short version of the Contributor Covenant, adapted for a small project.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in moltarc a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic
status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity
and orientation.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes: using welcoming and inclusive language, being
respectful of differing viewpoints, gracefully accepting constructive
criticism, focusing on what is best for the project, and showing empathy.

Unacceptable behavior includes: sexualized language or imagery, trolling,
insulting or derogatory comments, personal or political attacks, public or
private harassment, publishing others' private information without explicit
permission, and any other conduct reasonably considered inappropriate in a
professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (repo, issues, pull requests, CI) and in
public spaces when representing the project.

## Enforcement

Violations may be reported through a private GitHub security advisory or an
issue on the repo (`https://github.com/arfoux/moltarc/issues`). Maintainers
will review, may warn, temporarily ban, or permanently remove contributors
who behave unacceptably, and are not obligated to explain moderation
decisions beyond what keeps the project safe. This is a small team: expect
fair, brief handling, not a tribunal.
